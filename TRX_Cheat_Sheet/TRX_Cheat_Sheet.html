High Row

Pistol Squat
Hold handles, lift one leg forward. Lower on standing leg as deep as possible. Straps assist balance.

Single Arm Row + Rotation
Single arm row — at the top, rotate torso open and reach free arm to ceiling. Control the twist back.

Mark 1
Lunge + Overhead Reach
Step into reverse lunge, simultaneously reach both arms overhead through the straps. Great hip flexor opener.

Mark 1
Squat
Face anchor, hold handles. Sit back and down, use straps for balance and depth assist. Drive through heels.

Mark 1–2
Bicep Curl
Face anchor, lean back, palms up. Curl hands to temples keeping elbows high. Lower slowly.
📷Add image tricep_extension.jpg
Mark 1–2
Tricep Extension
Face away, handles overhead. Lean forward, bend elbows so forehead moves toward hands. Extend arms back.
Group 2 Marks 4 – 6 Straps mid-length — handles at roughly waist to chest height
📷Add image supine_row.jpg
Mark 4
Supine Row
Lie on back, hold handles overhead, heels on floor. Pull chest up to handles keeping body straight.
📷Add image chest_press.jpg
Mark 4–5
Chest Press
Face away from anchor, handles at chest. Lean forward, lower chest between handles, press back up.
📷Add image kneeling_pull.jpg
Mark 4–5
Kneeling Pull
Kneel facing anchor, handles high. Pull body forward and up keeping core braced.
📷Add image overhead_pull.jpg
Mark 5
Overhead Pull
Lean away from anchor, arm extended overhead. Pull arm down and through. Targets lat and oblique.
📷Add image lat_pull.jpg
Mark 5
Lat Pull
Arms extended overhead holding handles. Pull arms down and back, driving elbows to hips. Squeeze lats at bottom.
📷Add image chest_fly.jpg
Mark 5
Chest Fly
Face away, arms wide and extended forward. Let them open to sides, then squeeze together at chest height.
📷Add image hamstring_curl.jpg
Mark 5–6
Hamstring Curl
Lie on back, heels in cradles. Bridge up, curl heels to glutes. Keep hips level throughout.
Group 3 Marks 6.5 – 7 Straps long — handles low, close to floor
📷Add image pike.jpg
Mark 6.5
Pike
Start in plank, feet in cradles. Drive hips up toward ceiling keeping legs straight.
📷Add image lateral_knee_tuckL.jpg
Mark 6.5
Lateral Knee Tuck
Plank with feet in cradles. Drive both knees together laterally toward one side. Return and repeat other side.
📷Add image mountain_climber.jpg
Mark 7
Mountain Climber
Plank with feet in cradles. Drive knees alternately toward chest. Keep hips from bouncing.
📷Add image lateral_lunge_touch_L.jpg
Mark 7
Lateral Lunge + Touch
Hold handles for balance. Step wide to one side, sink into lunge, touch the floor. Drive back to center.
