My Unbreakable Weekly Cheat Sheet

Based on Dr. Vonda Wright's FACE Method · Pick your week type · Sequence blocks following the rules · Watch overrides everything

Block Legend

F Flexibility — Dynamic warm-up before + static stretches after every session
A Aerobic — Running at Zone 2 pace (conversational). Add SIT = 4 × 30 sec sprints at end
C1 Lower Body — Squat, deadlift, lunges, glutes, calves
C2 Upper Body — Push-ups, rows, shoulder press, biceps, triceps
C3 Full Body / Core — Planks, KB swings, Pallof press, balance. Lightest C block
E Equilibrium & Footspeed — Agility drills after running, before cool-down. ~10 min
M Movement Snacks — 10 squats/hour · single-leg balance · stair jumps · stand every 30–60 min

Sequencing Rules

Watch first72h+ recovery → C3 or M only. No exceptions.
After R+SITNever R or C the next day. Buffer with C3 or rest.
C daysNever same C block two days in a row. Max 2 C days consecutive.
RunningMorning only. Protects evening cortisol.
SITMax 1×/week. Always 4 sprints only — never 8.
Long runBonus only when watch is green + energy is genuinely high.
